AllPtimisation LLC

AllPtimisation LLC runs the operating work of the online store France Croquettes, and keeps a written record of that work.

Day to day

  • Sales, profitability, and the real cost of delivery.
  • Advertising and customer acquisition.
  • Product pages, the catalogue, and the articles on the store.
  • Order analytics, invoicing, and the monthly operating report.

Customer

The customer is France Croquettes, a French online retailer of dog food. The work is an ongoing operating relationship with that company.

Revenue

The company earns its revenue by invoicing France Croquettes for this operating, reporting, and analysis work.

How the work is recorded

Each day's work is written into a daily log. Those exports are read and summarized with software and AI, so the record of what was done stays usable.
